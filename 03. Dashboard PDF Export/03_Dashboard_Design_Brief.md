## 🎨 2. Detailed Visual Specifications per Sheet

### 📍 Sheet 1: Marketing Funnel
* **Focus**: Campaign acquisition efficiency, channel attribution and lead conversion.
* **Key Visuals**:
  1. **KPI Cards**: `Total Leads Generated`, `Total Marketing Spend ($)`, `Average Cost Per Lead (CPL)`.
  2. **Horizontal Bar Chart**: `Leads Generated by Channel` (LinkedIn, Google Ads, Email, Events) sorted descending.
  3. **Scatter Plot / Bubble Chart**: `Campaign Spend vs. Lead Conversion Rate` to identify highest ROI campaigns.
* **Controls**: Date Range Filter (`Start_Date`), Channel Dropdown Control.

---

### 📍 Sheet 2: Sales Pipeline
* **Focus**: Deal stage progression, revenue forecast by market segment and win/loss velocity.
* **Key Visuals**:
  1. **KPI Cards**: `Total Closed Won Revenue ($)`, `Active Pipeline Value ($)`, `Overall Win Rate (%)`.
  2. **Stacked Bar Chart**: `Deal Volume & Revenue by Stage` (*Prospecting*, *Qualification*, *Proposal*, *Negotiation*, *Closed Won*).
  3. **Line Chart**: `Monthly Closed Won Revenue Trend`.
  4. **Donut Chart**: `Revenue Distribution by Market Segment` (*Enterprise*, *Mid-Market*, *SMB*).
* **Interactivity**:
  * **One-Click Filtering**: Clicking a segment on the Donut Chart dynamically filters the Stage Bar Chart and Monthly Trend.
  * **Filter Controls**: Sales Rep Dropdown, Deal Stage Multi-Select.

---

### 📍 Sheet 3: Customer Health (Unified Insights)
* **Focus**: Post-sale account risk, customer satisfaction (CSAT), and support ticket severity.
* **Key Visuals**:
  1. **KPI Cards**: `Total Open Tickets`, `Average Resolution Time (Hours)`, `Average CSAT Score (out of 5.0)`.
  2. **Bar Chart**: `Support Ticket Distribution by Priority` (*Critical*, *High*, *Medium*, *Low*).
  3. **Cross-Dataset Visual (from Unified Dataset)**: **Account Risk Matrix (Scatter Plot)** plotting `Account Deal Value ($)` (X-axis) vs. `Open Critical Support Tickets` (Y-axis), colored by `CSAT Score`.
* **Interactivity & Navigation**:
  * **Cross-Sheet Navigation Action**: Clicking on an at-risk account in the scatter plot triggers a navigation action linking the user directly to the **Sales Pipeline Sheet** to view the account's historical deal history.

## 🖨️ 3. Publishing & PDF Export Workflow

1. In the analysis editor, click **Share** $\rightarrow$ **Publish dashboard**.
2. Name the dashboard: `NovaTech Revenue Intelligence Dashboard - Nihal N`.
3. Enable **Executive Summary** in the publish settings.
4. Click **Print / Export to PDF**, selecting **All sheets**.
5. Save the generated 3-page PDF file for submission.
